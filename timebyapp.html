App usage data

When you grant Android Usage Access, TimeByApp reads app identifiers and usage records from Android. It uses this information to calculate how much time you spend in each app and to display your usage history.

Storage and sharing

TimeByApp processes and stores usage history on your device. The app does not send usage history to the developer or share it with advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

Retention and deletion

Saved usage history is kept in the app's local storage. You can remove it by clearing TimeByApp's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Android manages its own usage records separately. If you grant Usage Access again, TimeByApp can read recent records that Android still makes available.

Control your permissions

You can revoke Usage Access for TimeByApp in Android Settings. This stops the app from reading additional usage records. You can clear the app's storage separately to remove saved history.

Google Play purchases

If you purchase TimeByApp through Google Play, Google Play processes the transaction. TimeByApp does not receive your payment card details.
